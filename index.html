The Idea

Most coaching fixes strokes.
I develop players — decision-making, adaptation, confidence under pressure, and an understanding of how you learn.

Technique matters. But progress depends on more than mechanics. My coaching draws on learning science, systems thinking, and a touch of Zen — so practice actually transfers to the moment a point is on the line.

Players don't just hit better shots. They start to see the game differently.

About Jason

Tennis is where the thinking becomes visible.

I'm a philosophy-trained, systems-minded coach. For 15+ years I've helped people learn — as a private tutor, an operations lead, and on the court. The same practice shows up everywhere: observe what's happening, find the friction, make a practical change, and help people adapt.

Tennis is the first place I've turned that into a structured system. I play ambidextrously, train with a Spinfire Pro 2 ball machine, and care less about a "correct" forehand than about helping you build a game that's genuinely yours.
